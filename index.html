      About
      
    
      It started in a pub. Someone noticed that Gloria Hunniford and Judith Chalmers were born impossibly close together. They weren't — 10 April 1940 and 10 October 1935 — but they were both born on the 10th, and after four pints that was enough. The game that followed: name someone born between two birthdays, and keep making the gap smaller.
      
    
      How it works
      
    
        
      
      • Ten levels. The gap between the two birthdays starts at three years and closes to ten days: three years, a year, six months, three months, two months, six weeks, a month, three weeks, a fortnight, ten days.
        • 
      
      • The year counts. Born on the same day as either anchor doesn't count. Strictly between.
        • 
      
      • Three lives. A wrong birthday costs one. A name we don't have costs nothing.
        • 
      
      • The list is the referee. It's a few thousand well-known people from Wikidata. If someone's missing, they're missing — argue in the pub, not with the website.
